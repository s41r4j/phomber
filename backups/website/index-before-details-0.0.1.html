GET STARTED

Three commands.
A clearer investigation.

Use the interactive terminal or run commands directly. Source-only research needs no AI key. AI assistance is optional and billed by your provider.

01 / INSTALL

One npm package.

Install globally. Prefer a one-off run? Use npx phomber instead.

npm install -g phomber
02 / INVESTIGATE

Start with what you know.

A guided questionnaire collects your context. This example keeps AI disabled.

phomber wiki --no-ai
03 / READ

Open your local wiki.

Serve past investigations, then open the printed URL in your browser.

phomber serve

THE TERMINAL, SIMPLIFIED

A small command set.
Room to go deeper.

Run phomber by itself to enter the interactive prompt. Inside it, omit the phomber prefix. Every command has focused help.

phomber wiki --no-aiGuided source-only investigation
phomber serveBrowse saved investigations
phomber reportsList reports and their IDs
phomber review REPORT_IDInspect finding assessments
phomber verify REPORT_IDCheck preserved evidence integrity
phomber number +442079460000Offline numbering-plan metadata
phomber dns example.comPublic DNS records
phomber whois example.comDomain registration via RDAP
phomber search "project"Search local research
phomber help scannersAll six scanners and examples

NPX follows the same pattern: npx phomber serve or npx phomber help. Add --open to serve only if you want the browser to open automatically.

FROM COLLECTION TO REVIEW

A result is a lead.
Keep the evidence.

Retrieving a page does not establish identity or prove a claim. PH0MBER keeps source collection, identity assessment and analyst decisions visible as separate steps.

Review excerpts in the wiki, record a reason for your decision, and compare preserved report states as your investigation changes.

CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Research responsibly.
Understand the limits.

Use public information with a legitimate purpose. Candidate accounts need human review, providers can be unavailable, and AI prose can be wrong. Partial coverage remains visible.

Responsible use ↗Report an issue ↗

Local workspace. Explicit external research.

Reports are stored on your computer. Public-source collection contacts external services. Wiki research uses billed DeepSeek AI by default; --no-ai disables it and --offline uses fresh cached evidence only.

This website is the project guide. Your investigation wiki runs separately on localhost. Phone tools provide numbering-plan metadata, not subscriber identity or live tracking. IP and domain tools provide registration information.

Gen2 is a local research tool. Authenticated team case management, PDF ingestion and broad registry connectors are outside this release.
