AN INVESTIGATION, NOT A GUESS

The finding matters.
So does how
you got there.

A matching username is a lead. A retrieved page is a source. Neither automatically proves who someone is or whether a claim is true.

Keep those distinctions visible, record your reasoning, and come back to the evidence when something changes.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good questions.
Clear boundaries.

Built for public-source research with human judgment. Understand the limits before turning a lead into a conclusion.

Do I need an AI subscription or API key?

Source-only research works without an AI key: use phomber wiki --no-ai. New wiki investigations use billed DeepSeek AI by default. AI research sends investigation context and selected public evidence to that provider. Use --offline for fresh cached evidence only; offline mode disables AI.

Does serve work without extra flags?

Yes. phomber serve starts the wiki and prints its URL. Open that address in your browser. --open is optional and opens the browser automatically. Press Ctrl+C to stop the server.

Where do my investigations live?

Installed and NPX commands use ~/.phomber unless you set PHOMBER_HOME. Checkout commands use the project's .phomber/ workspace. Use the same workspace when researching and serving. This public website does not host your investigation wiki.

Does a match confirm identity or ownership?

No. Matching handles are candidate leads. Registration data and MAC prefixes do not identify a person or device owner. Phone tools provide numbering-plan metadata, not subscriber identity or live location. Source credibility and AI prose still need analyst review.

What is included in Gen2 version 0.0.1?

The CLI, six scanner equivalents, public-source collection, optional AI research, local wiki, analyst reviews, preserved evidence, revision history, local search and portable exports. Authenticated team case management, PDF ingestion and broad registry connectors are outside this release.
