# Responsible public-source research

[Documentation index](README.md) · [Project guide](../README.md)

Use PH0MBER only with legitimate authority and respect source terms, privacy, intellectual-property restrictions, and applicable law. Do not bypass authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs or private profiles. Do not import leaked credentials, private contact details, precise residential locations or nonpublic personal information. Explicitly published professional contacts and general city/region/country may be retained from subject-linked first-party sources with citations; honor investigator omissions and review before sharing. Do not speculate about sensitive personal characteristics or private relationships.

Review ambiguous identities manually. A username, common name, collaborator mention or name search hit does not establish identity or a private relationship. Sources may contain outdated, inaccurate or malicious self-reported material. Findings preserve provenance and uncertainty; “verified” describes resource retrieval, not proof of a person's identity.

Reports are private and local by default. Directories are created mode 0700 and files mode 0600 on POSIX; Windows uses the user's inherited ACLs. Reports contain personal research and are not encrypted. Exports inherit destination directory permissions; protect them appropriately. Reading and download routes are read-only. The local wiki enables profile/collection management and explicitly confirmed report deletion without a login. Management forms require Origin checks and CSRF tokens. The localhost server accepts loopback Host headers, and exposes only allowlisted report files. Other processes on your computer may reach localhost. Do not proxy it publicly. There are no analytics. AI research is enabled by default for the find command and sends investigation context and public evidence to the configured provider. Use --no-ai or --offline to disable AI.

Use `--omit location` or another top-level subject field before collecting; omission also removes that field when resuming. Do not use omitted identifiers in other free-text fields. For erasure use `phomber reports delete ID --yes` and `phomber cache clear --yes`, and remove separately exported copies. Generated JSON embeds investigation input and evidence; review before sharing. Cache redaction cannot guarantee the removal of every personal detail from arbitrary biography text.

Historical Git archives contain old configuration data. The distributable Python ZIP excludes the known credential-bearing configuration. The legacy ZIP and Git history remain historical records. Checked-out legacy text may receive presentation corrections; rotate previously committed credentials and assess repository history separately.

Preserved report revisions and snapshots can retain earlier identifiers after `--omit`. Remove the complete report directory, related cache, separately exported copies and any derived search index when erasing an investigation. Deletion removes the selected report history; subsequent CLI search reconciles the derived index. Separate exports, response cache, catalog migration backups and deletion markers remain. This is not secure erasure of storage media. Analyst names in local review records are attribution labels, not authenticated identities or digital signatures. See [the analyst workflow](ANALYST_WORKFLOW.md).

## Email checks and future privacy cleanup

Use `phomber email ADDRESS` only for your own details or addresses whose owners authorize the check. It displays an authorization notice and masked output, and checks common syntax plus public mail-domain DNS. DNS requests use the domain rather than transmitting the mailbox local part. No SMTP probe, test message, ownership verification, breach lookup or contact harvesting is performed. The notice is not a consent-verification system. Public-source information can be incorrect, outdated or copied; being publicly accessible does not grant permission to contact or republish it.

Company workflows, authorized privacy exposure audits, precise-address handling and official verified-owner breach services are [planned work](TODO.md). Matching marks are heuristic evidence-association scores; do not interpret them as a probability, an identity certificate or a factual guarantee.
