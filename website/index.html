01Quick start

Full installation guide ↗

Three commands take you from a terminal to a readable investigation wiki. Source-only research needs no AI key, Python environment or Docker container.

1

Install PH0MBER.

Install once, then run it from any terminal.

npm install -g phomber
2

Build an investigation.

The questionnaire collects identifiers and context. For direct input, use phomber find "Full Name". This example keeps AI disabled.

phomber find --no-ai
3

Read your local wiki.

Start the server and open the printed localhost URL to browse saved investigations.

phomber serve

Node.js 22+ is required for npm and NPX. Check package status ↗

03Evidence & identity

Responsible use ↗

An investigation should make its reasoning inspectable. A page can be retrieved successfully while its identity match remains unresolved and its claims still need review.

Keep the distinctions visible. Inspect excerpts, record an analyst decision and the reason behind it, then revisit the review if the supporting evidence changes.

Reports retain source metadata and sanitized snapshots where available. Compare earlier states, check retained-content integrity and export portable HTML and JSON.

Hashes help detect content changes. They do not establish authenticity or factual truth. AI-generated prose still requires human judgment.

01

Collect within bounds

Public APIs, request budgets, bounded pagination and visible source failures.

02

Preserve supporting evidence

Source excerpts, sanitized snapshots, retrieval dates and SHA-256 checks.

03

Review the finding

Separate extraction, identity and claim assessments with evidence-bound analyst decisions.

04

Revisit & export

Search investigations, compare revisions and keep portable report folders.

Connecting facts across sources

Distinct matching education, employers, projects, publications and qualifications add heuristic marks. Shared-publisher pages and identical retained text do not provide extra confirmation. A probable match needs at least 70 marks, multiple strong clue families and two independently grouped sources. Identity conflicts block automatic expansion. Marks are not a percentage probability or verification of a CV claim.

Public documents, in context

Mixed online research can extract text from public PDFs locally, including CVs and resumes, and compare professional details with other sources. Parsing is bounded by size, page count and time. Later text chunks are considered; scanned image-only files require OCR, which is planned. DeepSeek-only mode does not use local PDF downloads.

04Common questions

Ask a question ↗
Do I need an AI subscription or API key?

Source-only research works without an AI key: use phomber find --no-ai. New wiki investigations use billed DeepSeek AI by default. AI research sends investigation context and selected public evidence to that provider. Use --offline for fresh cached evidence only; offline mode disables AI.

How do I use reports and delete an investigation?

phomber reports prints a readable list. Use phomber reports delete REPORT_ID for a terminal confirmation, or choose Delete report in the local wiki. Scripts require --yes. Add --format json when you need machine-readable output. Separate exports and shared response cache remain after report deletion.

How are repeated investigations organized?

Matching supplied or reviewed profile identifiers, or a supplied name plus shared context, can group research runs into versions automatically. Names alone and ambiguous or conflicting matches stay separate. Profile history explains the grouping and provides split/move controls. Evidence and factual assessments stay separate for each version.

Does serve work without extra flags?

Yes. phomber serve starts the wiki and prints its URL. Open that address in your browser. --open is optional and opens the browser automatically. Press Ctrl+C to stop the server.

Where do my investigations live?

Installed, NPX and checkout commands share reports, settings, cache and saved keys in ~/.phomber on macOS/Linux or %USERPROFILE%\.phomber on Windows. Use phomber config or npx phomber config to see the resolved workspace. Set PHOMBER_HOME to the same absolute path in each terminal to choose another folder. Data stays on this device; it is not synced across computers. This public website does not host your investigation wiki.

Does a match confirm identity or ownership?

No. Matching handles are candidate leads. Registration data and MAC prefixes do not identify a person or device owner. Phone tools provide numbering-plan metadata, not subscriber identity or live location. Source credibility and AI prose still need analyst review.

Does an email check prove ownership or breach exposure?

No. Use phomber email ADDRESS only for your own information or an authorized subject. It checks common syntax and public mail-domain DNS, masks the address and makes no mailbox-existence, ownership or breach claim. Privacy cleanup and verified-owner breach services are planned. Public availability does not establish accuracy or permission to contact or republish personal information.

What is included in Gen2 version 0.0.2?

The CLI, six legacy scanner equivalents, an email syntax/DNS check, public-source collection, optional AI research, weighted cross-source matching, text-based public PDF extraction, a local wiki, automatic profile versions, analyst reviews, preserved evidence and portable exports. Dedicated company research, privacy cleanup, address auditing, breach services and OCR are planned work.

What comes next

PLANNED

The items below are a roadmap, not available features. The current local checkout is Gen2 0.0.2; registry packages and the deployed website may lag it.

Company & organization research

A dedicated workflow for legal entity names, registration identifiers, official domains, brands, products and dated organizational relationships. Current person-focused research does not provide a complete company investigation workflow.

Authorized privacy cleanup

Planned: verified ownership or consent, an inventory of public email/address exposure, masked display, source dates and removal tracking. Official breach-notification services are a future verified-owner integration. No password dumps or breach contents are retrieved. The available email command checks supplied syntax and mail-domain DNS only.

Broader document coverage

Text-based public PDFs have bounded local extraction today. OCR for image-only scans, additional formats such as DOCX, and live authorized matching benchmarks are planned.
